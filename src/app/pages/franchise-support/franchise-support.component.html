FRANCHISE SUPPORT

When you own a franchise with énergie Fitness, you'll benefit from one of the most comprehensive franchise business support packages available on the market. Our award-winning UK-based operations team have a 20-year track record of success, and are there to provide advice and guidance every step of the way.

So what's included?

1. Business planning and finance

We'll work with you to develop a robust business plan that supports profitability, and can help raise additional funding through our partnerships with major banks.

2. Site selection

Working with our property partners, will help you to find and secure the right site, assisting with lease negotiations and helping you plan the fit out.

3. Supplier discounts

Benefit from our relationships with industry-leading suppliers for gym equipment, signage, marketing materials and membership management solutions.

4. Management course

Receive training on every aspect of running a gym franchise business with our 3-day énergie Basic Management Course (EBMC).

5. Operations set up

From HR policies and operational handbooks through to our innovative business management software, we have everything you need to get up and running.

6. énergie Training Academy

Initial and ongoing training for staff to ensure smooth operation of your business as well as supporting you and your team's ongoing professional development.

7. Pre-sales and launch

We help you to plan and implement your pre-sales marketing campaigns.

8. Central marketing support

Gain an advantage over your local competition through powerful centrally-created marketing campaigns.
